Easy Recipe Generator

Kitchen full of ingredients but you don't know what to cook? Use this AI-powered recipe generator to find simple and delicious recipes using ingredients you already have. Enter your ingredients below and within seconds a recipe will be generated for you.

i.e. chicken, tofu, carrots, etc. You can enter more than one ingredient.
This AI project was coded by Chelsea Peterdy, is open-sourced on Github and is hosted on Netlify
